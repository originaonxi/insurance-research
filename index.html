02 / The truths no one tells you

5 things buried in policy documents that decide your claim.

Health
01 / Room Rent Trap
One room upgrade. Bills cut everywhere.
Booked a ₹6K/day room. Policy says ₹3K limit. Insurer cuts room rent AND applies proportional reduction on doctor fees, ICU, medicines — everything. Not just the room.
Auto
02 / Flood Engine Trick
You drove. Engine died. Not covered.
Drove through a waterlogged road. Engine seized. That's "consequential loss" — not covered. Only the flood damage before you moved is covered. Every monsoon, thousands claim this and lose.
Health
03 / The 24-Hour Trap
Called the hospital. Forgot the insurer.
You must notify the insurer within 24–48 hours of hospitalisation. Emergency at 2 AM, phone dead, family panicking — doesn't matter. Miss this window = procedural rejection. Thousands of valid claims denied this way.
Life
04 / The Non-Disclosure Void
Forgot to mention blood pressure. Policy voided.
Didn't disclose diabetes or blood pressure in the proposal? After death, insurer investigates medical history and can void the entire policy — even after 10 years of regular premium payments.
Auto
05 / The NCB Math
One small claim. Five years of bonus gone.
One claim resets your No Claim Bonus (up to 50% discount) back to zero. A ₹8K repair? You lose a ₹12K annual discount. Often better to pay from pocket than claim.
